Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

How does ShopIntel extract prices when websites block bots? ▼
ShopIntel uses a multi-tier proxy architecture with real browser fingerprint headers (Brotli/Gzip decoding, mobile user-agents, Google referrers) and parses server-side embedded JSON-LD schema or hydrated window state variables before anti-scraping scripts trigger.
Can I compare products between completely different websites? ▼
Yes! You can paste a link from Amazon.in, a link from Flipkart, and a link from Puma.com all in the same comparison session. ShopIntel normalizes their currency, ratings, and specifications into a uniform side-by-side comparison table.
How is the ShopIntel AI Score (0-100) calculated? ▼
The algorithm scores each item based on 6 key factors: verified star rating (up to 40 pts), price competitiveness relative to other items in the batch (up to 25 pts), discount percentage off genuine MRP (up to 15 pts), brand credibility, customer review volume, and delivery convenience.
Is ShopIntel completely free, and is my data private? ▼
Yes, 100% free and open-source. All search history, wishlist items, and personal preferences remain stored in your local browser (localStorage). We do not sell your shopping history or track you across the internet.
Do I need a Google Gemini API key to use ShopIntel? ▼
No! The default HTML extraction engine works completely without any API key. Google Gemini 2.0 Flash is an optional fallback mode that you can activate if you are comparing rare or unsupported boutique e-commerce stores.
